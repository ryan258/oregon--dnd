# 1. Events & Hazards

At the start of each Trail Day, the GM rolls 1d20 on the Universal Event Table (see `seed.md`). If the result directs you to the Segment-Specific table, or if the GM prefers more local flavor, roll **1d10** on the table matching your current segment.

---

### Segment 1: The Plains (Miles 0–300)

_The great flat expanse. The primary threats are weather, initial inexperience, and the sheer shock of the undertaking._

| d10 | Event                      | Mechanical Effect                                                                                          |
| :-: | :------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Sudden Thunderstorm**    | Lightning spooks the oxen. DEX check (DC 13) or 1 ox team takes 2 HP damage. Travel speed halved this day. |
|  2  | **Heavy Spring Mud**       | The wagon bogs down to the axles. STR check (DC 15, group) to dig it out, or lose a travel day.            |
|  3  | **First Signs of Illness** | A random character makes a CON save (DC 12). Failure: Gain 1 Exhaustion level.                             |
|  4  | **Prairie Fire (Distant)** | The smoke chokes the air. Visibility drops. Disadvantage on Navigation/Hunting for 2 days.                 |
|  5  | **Misjudged Rations**      | Party realizes food was packed poorly. Lose 1d4 x 10 lbs of food to spoilage.                              |
|  6  | **Swarm of Mosquitoes**    | Miserable conditions. Morale drops by 1 unless characters have protective clothing.                        |
|  7  | **Friendly Emigrant Pass** | Meet another wagon heading back. They trade 1 Medicine for 20 lbs of food, or vice versa.                  |
|  8  | **Buffalo Sign**           | The Hunter finds fresh tracks. Next Hunting check has Advantage.                                           |
|  9  | **Clear, Cool Day**        | Perfect marching weather. Travel progress counts as 1.5x for the day.                                      |
| 10  | **Stunning Sunset**        | The vastness of the prairie inspires hope. Party Morale increases by 1.                                    |

### Segment 2: The River Crossings (Miles 300–500)

_The Kansas and Platte rivers. The primary threats are drowning, losing the wagon, and waterborne disease._

| d10 | Event                   | Mechanical Effect                                                                                                           |
| :-: | :---------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Treacherous Ford**    | The riverbed gives way. Wagon takes 1d6 damage. Drowning risk for anyone walking (STR DC 13).                               |
|  2  | **Ferry Bottleneck**    | A 3-day wait for the ferry, or the party can risk fording a dangerous section immediately (DC 16).                          |
|  3  | **Cholera Scare**       | Passing a fresh grave farm. Roll 1d6 for disease (Cholera on a 3-4, Dysentery otherwise).                                   |
|  4  | **Saturating Rains**    | Everything gets wet. Wood is useless. No fire tonight. Sleep in the cold (CON DC 11 or 1 Exhaustion).                       |
|  5  | **Lost Footing**        | An ox slips in the current. Requires immediate DEX check (DC 14) by the Teamster to save the beast from taking 4 HP damage. |
|  6  | **Washed Up Supplies**  | Find a barrel from a wrecked wagon upstream. Contains 50 lbs of unspoiled flour.                                            |
|  7  | **Shallow Crossing**    | The river is unseasonably low here. Crossing is an Easy (DC 8) check.                                                       |
|  8  | **Ferry Operator Deal** | The operator offers passage for spare parts instead of cash.                                                                |
|  9  | **Abundant Fish**       | Camp by an eddy. Free 2d10 lbs of fish without expending ammunition.                                                        |
| 10  | **Safe Calm Night**     | A rare dry, quiet evening by the river. All characters recover 1 extra HP during rest.                                      |

### Segment 3: The Rockies Approach (Miles 500–700)

_The land rises, the air thins, and the oxen begin to suffer. Wood and grass become scarce._

| d10 | Event                     | Mechanical Effect                                                                                               |
| :-: | :------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Broken Axle on Stone**  | A hidden rock snaps the axle. Cost: 1 Spare Part and 1 day to repair.                                           |
|  2  | **Lame Ox**               | The rocky ground batters hooves. 1 ox team loses 3 HP and party speed drops 25% until healed.                   |
|  3  | **Alkali Water**          | Toxic pools. Anyone drinking makes CON DC 15 or suffers severe vomiting (lose 1d4 HP, Disadvantage on STR/DEX). |
|  4  | **False Trail**           | A braided path confuses the Guide. WIS DC 14 to realize the error, otherwise lose 2 days.                       |
|  5  | **Hailstorm**             | Marbles of ice batter the party. No shelter means 1d4 damage to everyone and 1d6 damage to the wagon cover.     |
|  6  | **Scare Wildlife**        | Hunting DC increases to 16 for the next 3 days. Game is hiding in the crags.                                    |
|  7  | **Buffalo Robe Trade**    | Encounter trappers willing to trade 3 thick robes for 50 rounds of ammunition.                                  |
|  8  | **Edible Roots**          | The Doctor recognizes distinct local flora. Forage 3d10 lbs of edible roots, no ammo needed.                    |
|  9  | **Clear Mountain Spring** | Pure, icy water restores 1 Exhaustion level to anyone who rests here for an hour.                               |
| 10  | **Independence Rock**     | You've made it in good time. Carve your names in the stone. Morale increases by 1.                              |

### Segment 4: South Pass (Miles 700–900)

_The Continental Divide. Biting winds, altitude sickness, and the psychological weight of the mountains._

| d10 | Event                     | Mechanical Effect                                                                                                                 |
| :-: | :------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Unexpected Blizzard**   | Whiteout conditions. Must make camp immediately. Lose 2 days. Cold checks (CON DC 14) each day.                                   |
|  2  | **Altitude Sickness**     | The thin air breaks the weak. CON save (DC 13). Failure: 2 Exhaustion levels and bedridden.                                       |
|  3  | **Steep Grade Ascent**    | The oxen refuse to pull. STR DC 15 (Group) to manually push the wagon over the crest, or retreat and try another route (+3 days). |
|  4  | **Night Freeze**          | Temperatures plummet. If party lacks winter gear, everyone takes 1d6 cold damage overnight.                                       |
|  5  | **Wolf Pack Signs**       | Stalking the oxen. Someone must stay awake on watch all night (gain 1 Exhaustion level) or lose an ox.                            |
|  6  | **Supply Drop**           | To make the ascent, the party MUST leave behind 200 lbs of weight (food, parts, or gear).                                         |
|  7  | **Sturdy Pine Patch**     | Excellent timber for repairs. Masterwork repair adds +5 HP to the wagon instead of usual.                                         |
|  8  | **Mountain Goat Sighted** | Difficult hunt (DEX DC 16) but yields heavy, fatty meat and warm pelts.                                                           |
|  9  | **The Divide Crossed**    | Water now flows west. A monumental milestone. Morale increases by 2.                                                              |
| 10  | **Clear Vistas**          | A rare break in the clouds reveals the path ahead. Next 3 Navigation checks have Advantage.                                       |

### Segment 5: Desert Crossing (Miles 900–1,100)

_The arid wastes beyond the pass. Heat, dehydration, and alkaline dust._

| d10 | Event                  | Mechanical Effect                                                                                          |
| :-: | :--------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Dried Up Well**      | The marked spring is dust. No water. Everyone takes 1 level of Exhaustion and 1d4 damage.                  |
|  2  | **Dust Storm**         | Choking, burning grit. CON DC 14 or suffer eye/lung irritation (Disadvantage on WIS/DEX for 2 days).       |
|  3  | **Rattlesnake Strike** | A snake in the camp gear. DEX save (DC 13) to avoid. Failure: 1d6 damage and Poisoned condition.           |
|  4  | **Sun Madness**        | The heat breaks morale. CHA DC 14 to stop a physical fight over water rations. Failure: Morale drops by 1. |
|  5  | **Warped Wood**        | The extreme dryness shrinks wagon wheels. They loosen dangerously. 1 day lost to soak/repair them.         |
|  6  | **Spoiled Meat**       | Heat ruins stored game. Lose half of all current food reserves.                                            |
|  7  | **Desert Hare**        | Scrawny but catchable. Easy hunt (DC 10) yields 10 lbs of stringy meat.                                    |
|  8  | **Oasis**              | A miraculous seep of water and shade. Full rest here counts double for HP recovery.                        |
|  9  | **Cool Cloudy Day**    | The sun hides. Travel requires half the usual water/food rationing.                                        |
| 10  | **Finding the River**  | You strike the Snake River a day early. Huge relief. Morale +1, Exhaustion reduced by 1.                   |

### Segment 6: The Snake River (Miles 1,100–1,400)

_Following the canyons. Difficult terrain, rushing rapids, and the final stretch of hostile geography._

| d10 | Event                    | Mechanical Effect                                                                                                  |
| :-: | :----------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Overturned Wagon**     | The trail hugs a cliff. A slip costs the wagon 1d10 HP and dumps 1d4x50 lbs of supplies into the river.            |
|  2  | **Rapids Ford**          | Forced to cross deep, fast water. High drowning risk. Group DEX save (DC 15). Failure means sweeping downstream.   |
|  3  | **Dysentery Outbreak**   | The water is foul. Roll 1d4 party members: they immediately contract Dysentery (CON DC 14 to resist).              |
|  4  | **Steep Ravine Descent** | Must lower the wagon by ropes. STR DC 16 (Extended: 3 successes before 2 fails). Failure: Wagon takes huge damage. |
|  5  | **Broken Yoke**          | The strain breaks the ox tackle. Stop for 1 day to carve a new one, or abandon 1 ox team.                          |
|  6  | **Stolen Stock**         | Horse thieves in the night. WIS (Perception) DC 15 to spot them. Failure: Lose $50 worth of goods or 1 ox.         |
|  7  | **Salmon Run**           | The river is thick with fish. A single DEX DC 10 check yields 100 lbs of food.                                     |
|  8  | **Ford Operator**        | A local offers to guide you across the treacherous ford for $10. Ensures safe crossing.                            |
|  9  | **Abandoned Cache**      | Find an intact barrel of salted pork buried by a previous party. (150 lbs food).                                   |
| 10  | **Smooth Sailing**       | The river banks widen, the trail flattens. A rare easy stretch. Make double time today.                            |

### Segment 7: The Blue Mountains (Miles 1,400–2,000)

_The final hurdle before the valley. Starvation, winter snows, and the brink of physical collapse._

| d10 | Event                          | Mechanical Effect                                                                                       |
| :-: | :----------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  1  | **The Bitter Cold**            | Winter is here. CON DC 15 every day. Failure means 1d4 cold damage and 1 Exhaustion level.              |
|  2  | **Starvation Sets In**         | Food is gone. Everyone takes 1d6 damage daily. Morale drops 1 point every 2 days.                       |
|  3  | **Fallen Trees**               | A storm dropped pines across the trail. Stop for 2 days to chop through them.                           |
|  4  | **The Axle Snaps**             | The final straw. The wagon is dead. Do you repair it (needs parts/str) or abandon it and walk the rest? |
|  5  | **Lost in the Pines**          | The trail vanishes under snow. Guide must make WIS DC 16 or wander aimlessly for 3 days.                |
|  6  | **Oxen Collapse**              | The beasts can't go further. 1 team dies immediately.                                                   |
|  7  | **Hunter's Luck**              | You track an elk in the deep snow. DEX DC 14. Success means 200 lbs of life-saving meat.                |
|  8  | **Descend below the Snowline** | You break through the clouds. The air warms. Remove 1 level of Exhaustion from all.                     |
|  9  | **Rescue Party**               | Columbia River locals come up the trail with fresh horses. Speed increases to 1.5x.                     |
| 10  | **Sight of the Valley**        | You see the Willamette Valley spread out green and gold below you. Morale +3. You're going to make it.  |
