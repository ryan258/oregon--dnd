## Cover prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: portrait front cover for the instructions book of a personal tabletop roleplaying game.
Create a beautifully composed finished book cover, portrait format, tactile woodcut and gouache illustration on warm ivory paper. Restrained forest green, indigo, ochre and burnt sienna palette. A small canvas-covered emigrant wagon and a pair of oxen on a winding trail, four ordinary adult travelers walking together, layered plains rising into distant mountains, an immense dusk sky, a river threading the valley. Fictional 1848 Oregon journey. Human-scale, thoughtful, quietly hopeful, weather and distance felt; no heroic conquest framing. Elegant restrained border, vintage naturalist field-guide quality.
Top third reserved for large exceptionally legible classic serif typography. Text verbatim: "OREGON TRAIL" and below it "A ROLEPLAYING GAME". Bottom text verbatim: "People. Promises. The road between." and "PLAYTEST EDITION • v0.10". Integrate typography into the actual cover. No other text. No brands, modern objects, guns as focal point, caricatures, magical fantasy, watermarks, or mockup book/spine: flat complete cover art edge-to-edge.

## Evening camp prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide landscape banner for the Oregon Trail RPG website, companion illustration to a warm-paper vintage naturalist book cover.
A quiet evening camp on a fictional 1848 overland journey: four ordinary adult travelers share a small fire beside a canvas covered wagon, two resting oxen nearby, a coffee pot, a wooden tool chest and a closed bundle of books. They are listening to one another, with space and dignity. Vast layered plains, river bend and distant blue mountains under the last amber light. Wide panoramic composition with camp and wagon toward the right and open landscape to the left. Tactile finely engraved woodcut lines and restrained gouache colors, warm ivory paper, forest green, indigo, ochre and burnt sienna. Thoughtful, intimate, quietly hopeful. Art fills the whole frame, no border. No text, letters, logos, mockup, weapons as focal point, modern objects, magical fantasy, or conquest imagery.
