# Playtest the experience — v0.10

**Status:** this edition implements a manuscript critique. No human table result is claimed for it. The changes are hypotheses about better choices, participation, variety, and recall. A local calculation or document check cannot establish those outcomes.

## First test: the river, as written

Use the [quick observation sheet](sheets/playtest_observer.md) for the first table. During play track only time to first consequential choice, rules interruptions, and participation gaps. An observer can take those notes; without one, the GM uses brief ticks and marks missing data unknown. Capture route/deadline response and each player’s owned choice in the five-minute debrief. Final state stays on the existing wagon and character sheets.

The detailed table below is a follow-up research form, not a requirement to monitor every measure live. Estimate accounting time afterward unless someone else can time it without interrupting play.

Use one GM, four pregens, and the exact river starting snapshot. Target 2–3 hours including debrief and record actual time and ending. End at the far-bank camp if needed. Read the four offers, the optional scouting day, and Nessa's announced departure from the [river decision card](sheets/river_decision_card.md) before commitment. Use the wagon sheet's Next decision panel to record any new quote or change in conditions. Let players decline personal hooks and choose the crossing they prefer.

Do not change supply amounts or damage mid-test to force tension. Record any necessary house ruling, including the reason and what changed. Table safety changes remain available at any time without a mechanical charge.

Did any cost feel arbitrary? Would you choose to play the next chapter, and why? Ask for a 1–10 rating only afterward; do not mention a target score.

## Acceptance targets, not results

- First consequential decision within 15 minutes of explanation starting.
- Each player identifies a choice they owned that changed time, resources, a relationship, an opportunity, or a route. Merely rolling their assigned check does not count.
- Players understand at least two options' different benefits before choosing. Fast agreement is fine; prolonging an argument is not a success criterion.
- Nobody loses participation because of illness, death, or replacement cost.
- No catastrophic consequence relies on an undisclosed risk.
- At least one scene is remembered for a person, pleasure, or changed relationship, not only a loss total.
- Record the accounting share as a baseline. Do not set a universal acceptable percentage before observing this table; fix any stretch players identify as draining.

Do not average away an excluded player. Do not make players perform doubt, gratitude, or sadness to pass a test. A quiet description counts.

## Check the travel card before a longer campaign

Give an independent GM the [travel procedure card](sheets/travel_procedure.md) and wagon sheet. Resolve one healthy Standard leg, then a leg from the desert stress snapshot below with illness and a weather change. Use actual rolled results; do not require a particular outcome. Record where the GM stopped, which rules they looked up, and which values they recalculated. Check that unused days stayed unspent and recovery was not applied backward. Separate rules mistakes from awkward presentation before proposing changes. These are usability exercises, not human results already obtained.

## Next tests, one at a time

1. **Independent GM:** a GM who has not discussed the rules runs the revised river packet without designer coaching. Record missing procedure, prep time, and actual use of the decision panel.
2. **Desert stress session:** use the setup below. Measure whether rest, information, supply planning, and learned techniques give meaningful choices without concealing the arithmetic.
3. **Earned finale:** use actual earlier people, promises, supplies, and techniques. Read both route offers, choose one, and finish its printed procedure and Arrival. If testing the other route, explicitly rewind a copy of the same state; do not merge both outcomes. Did someone from earlier play change the decision or future? Does a safely purchased carrier journey still feel earned?
4. **Full campaign:** record real duration, repeated-event fatigue, chapters where each role mattered, food/water margin, decisions to Rest, and memorable joyful scenes. New NPCs and techniques remain provisional until encountered in play.

### Desert stress snapshot

Constructed test setup, not an earlier campaign result: **July 10, marker 900**, at the full departure spring; Hot and Dry weather has three days remaining. Wagon 12/30; one living usable team 6/10, other teams absent; food 80 lbs cured, water **40/40 gallons**, medicine 2, parts 1, ammunition 15, cash $20, Warm Bedding and ordinary tools. Use pregens at 9/9 HP except Cal at 5/9. Eliza and Cal each have Exhaustion 2; Cal has dysentery, 3 recovery marks, 1 damage due today. Others are healthy. Morale 5; no threshold loss already used this chapter; one Inspiration each. All four can act. Each player chooses **one** technique earned after chapter 2; chapter 5's second technique is not yet earned.

Record actual chosen techniques, no invented favors, no personal cargo loss. The departure spring can refill freely while here; do not pretend the initial supply cannot be replenished. Show the initial Standard speed: ceil(18 ×0.75 ×0.5) = **7 miles/day**. Forty gallons in heat cover 10 days at that speed, fewer than the 13 unchanged travel days needed to the 90-mile spring. This forecast assumes no recovery or further hazard; daily recovery or a Rest Day can change it. Let players propose the response. Use the chapter's sources and live weather/events, not a secretly dried-up confirmed spring.

### Economy observations across chapters

At each chapter entry/exit record food, cash, elapsed days, actual people fed, successful hunts, spoilage, donations, purchases, Rest Days, and chosen stance. Ask the Hunter which food decision mattered. Keep the v0.10 starter allowance unchanged for the first campaign; a later experiment should alter one parameter and identify its edition.

The core's 700 lbs = 175 ordinary four-person ration days; ceil(2,000/18) = 112 is only a no-delay comparison, not a playable itinerary (fort stops, weather, final-route durations, and risks differ). At the standalone DC 12 ford, using each pregen's best eligible ability without penalties, chances of **no successes** are 5.0625%, or 0.2562890625% if everyone spends Inspiration. Those are not chances of harm: partial success still costs wagon HP and cargo or injury. Use these calculations as questions for play, never as proof of balance.
