# Playtest the experience — v0.9

**Status:** constructed examples and local document checks are not human playtests. No external-table result is claimed. This edition aims for a 9/10 experience; the following observations decide what still needs changing.

## Bounded first test

Run The River Between with one GM and the four pregens. Aim for 2–3 hours including a short debrief, but record actual time and where play ended. Use the standard rules and starting snapshot. No house-rule rescue without writing down the problem and intervention. Players can always exercise safety boundaries.

Ask privately if possible: Did you understand the risk before choosing? Did your role matter? Did another player's decisions change yours? Did any failure feel arbitrary? Would you choose to play the next chapter, and why? Invite a 1–10 rating only after these answers; don't tell players they are validating a nine.

## Acceptance targets, not current claims

- First consequential decision within 15 minutes of beginning explanation.
- Every player names at least one choice they owned that changed time, resources, a relationship, or a route.
- No player loses participation because their PC is sick, dead, or unaffordable to replace.
- No catastrophic consequence surprises players with a risk they could not have known.
- Common actions resolved from the packet; every ambiguous lookup becomes a correction ticket.
- Players remember a specific person/promise/choice and voluntarily want to continue.

Do not average away a player who felt excluded. A high overall rating cannot conceal a broken participation rule.

## Next tests, only after revising the first

1. **Independent GM:** someone who has not discussed the rules runs the river packet without designer coaching. Record where they invented missing procedure.
2. **Desert stress session:** start at 900 with one usable team, reduced supplies, an active condition, and the actual player-selected techniques. Verify all route costs are visible, water planning works, and recovery remains interesting.
3. **Earned finale:** carry actual people/promises/resources from earlier play into both route options. Does earlier help alter the decision? Does each player author their ending?
4. **Full campaign:** measure real session count, repeated-event fatigue, food/water surplus or shortage, and how often each role's abilities matter.

Run one bounded test at a time. No need for a large simulator or full digital platform until a specific play problem warrants it. A statistical survival estimate alone cannot verify agency, pacing, or emotional payoff.
