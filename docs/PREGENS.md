# Five people, one wagon

*A guide carrying her dead mother's books. A doctor who keeps an unpaid bill in his pocket. A hunter who will not risk a person for property. A teamster with her father's tools and her name on a sign that does not exist yet. A steward who keeps a kettle warm so no one goes quiet at the fire.*

They have already agreed to share the wagon. They have not yet found out what that will cost. Hand these out as they are and play — everything is filled in, and the only question at the top of the first session is the one printed under each name.

Version 0.10. Use these directly. Each uses scores 15, 13, 12, 10, 10, 8 exactly once; all modifiers and HP are filled. No techniques at campaign start. Each has one Inspiration, no wounds, Exhaustion 0. Rename or revise any identity/personal statement before play. The other player must agree to a Bond. All are fictional adults.

Campaign supplies: core starter loadout, **$250 remaining**. Standalone river supplies: use that adventure's separate starting snapshot instead. Personal cargo has no sale price; it becomes a named bundle for choices. Each keepsake is small and carried personally.

## Eliza Marsh — Guide

| 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 8 (−1) | 9/9 |

**Background:** Cartographer. Read the ground also identifies a usable sheltered campsite or safe water source on the chosen route before the next known major obstacle.

**Expertise:** marked-route navigation DC 12 or lower succeeds unless the hazard obscures it. Read the ground spends one travel day to reveal both routes' immediate costs and hazards; no navigation check at that junction.

**Dream:** open a school where my late mother's books will be used. **Promise:** deliver those books intact. **Boundary:** I will not abandon someone because they slow us down. **Bond:** Mae tells me when my optimism is endangering the wagon. **Cargo:** book crate, one named bundle. **Keepsake:** mother's handwritten lesson list. **Voice:** gives distances when frightened.

**Opening question:** Do you want people to trust your judgment, or to share your dream?

**On the road:** Mercy will ask to put a book to use before Oregon. Decide whether lending it changes your promise. No gift is presumed.

**Reserve:** Jonah Reed, willing to help after traveling with Mercy; takes the Guide role if needed, no free gear.

## Thomas Pruitt — Doctor

| 8 (−1) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 9/9 |

**Background:** Sanitation Keeper. Once per chapter, grant all exposed travelers Advantage against one event's initial disease resistance before rolling.

**Expertise:** identify a patient's game condition/risk without a roll once daily. Stabilize on a Rest Day uses 1 medicine plus Camp Action and daily treatment: prevent that patient's condition damage/worsening today and remove 2 marks from one condition instead of its recovery roll.

**Dream:** begin a practice where people can ask for care without being ashamed of debt. **Promise:** keep enough medicine to help Cal reach the Valley. **Boundary:** I will not promise a cure I cannot provide. **Bond:** Cal knows the patient I could not save back home. **Keepsake:** an unpaid bill kept as a reminder. **Voice:** asks permission before touching.

**Opening question:** When does saving supplies become refusing care?

**On the road:** Ada asks for care at the river; a freight caretaker later asks to learn from you. Decide what you can offer while keeping Cal in mind.

**Reserve:** Ellis Bell, a carrier with practical nursing experience; takes Doctor role if needed.

**Background:** Sharpshooter. Ordinary hunts cost 3 ammunition instead of 5. Take Aim still costs 5.

**Expertise:** Take Aim uses a full Hunt Day and 5 ammunition for 30 lbs fresh food where game is available. Stretch the stores once per chapter halves five travelers' next three days of food, rounded daily to 0.5 lb; cannot create food at zero.

**Dream:** earn a living without hunting for someone else's profit. **Promise:** help Eliza's school survive its first winter. **Boundary:** I will not risk a person merely to save property. **Bond:** Thomas has seen me frightened and did not use it against me. **Keepsake:** a carved wooden bird. **Voice:** makes jokes while doing practical work.

**Opening question:** Who gets the last portion when you are the one who found it?

**On the road:** Mercy may need food; Nessa later offers paid provisioning work. Decide what a fair reserve and independent livelihood mean to you.

**Reserve:** Rowan Hale, another wagon's hunter; player defines the existing connection.

## Clara Vance — Steward

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA | HP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 (−1) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 9/9 |

**Background:** Church Choir & Supper. Once per chapter, when the party shares a Proper Meal or rests together on a Rest Day, restore 1 extra Morale (max 10).

**Expertise:** Fair Bargain once per settlement or major crossing reduces one cash purchase, service fee, or toll by 20% (minimum $1 off, rounded up) without a roll; in camp, any traveler may spend a quiet evening with you to speak honestly without a CHA check. Hold the Wagon Together once per chapter cancels 1 point of Morale loss from an event, shortage, or hard choice.

**Dream:** open a boarding house in Oregon City where travelers can sit down to a hot meal and a quiet room. **Promise:** bring my late husband's cast-iron kettle and keep this wagon talking to each other until we arrive. **Boundary:** I will not let someone be left out of a decision just because they are quiet or tired. **Bond:** Thomas knows I am still grieving, and he lets me help without making me explain. **Keepsake:** husband's brass pocket watch that no longer ticks. **Voice:** pours tea or water before anyone starts arguing.

**Opening question:** Who in this wagon do you worry about most when everyone goes quiet?

**On the road:** At the river, Ada's schoolteacher friend and the ferryman are both watching how your wagon treats people who are short on coin. Decide what fairness looks like when your own purse is thin.

**Reserve:** Martha Holt, a widowed seamstress traveling two wagons back; takes the Steward role if needed, no free gear.

## At chapter end

Nobody gains hit points on this road. What changes is what they believe.

Ask each player whether a personal statement changes. At chapters 2 and 5 choose a different [technique](../02_character_backgrounds.md), copy it onto the sheet, and name who/what taught it. At incapacitation or death use core substitution rules immediately; no reserve character grants an extra active action or starting supply package.
