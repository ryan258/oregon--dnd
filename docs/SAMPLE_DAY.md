# Worked procedures — v0.10

Worked moments played out in full, so the arithmetic is never the thing slowing a table down. Read them when a rule feels ambiguous, not before your first session.

These are constructed examples, not observed playtest results. Use the [core](../seed.md) when resolving a new interaction.

## 1. A river decision that costs something

Use The River Between's standalone snapshot: June 2, marker 340, food 140 cured, wagon 22, three teams 8 HP each, money$25, medicine 4, parts 2. Weather Clear and Mild. All five travelers are healthy, full 9 HP, Exhaustion 0.

The group chooses the upper ford immediately. Before dice the GM reads DC 12 and all three outcomes. Players make DEX/WIS checks: Eliza total 15, Thomas 8, Cal 13, Mae 9, Clara 10. **Two successes among five is partial**, not full success. They cross; wagon loses 2 HP. They choose losing Mae's tool chest instead of a volunteer taking 1d4 damage. Mae decides her dream survives but she must borrow tools to begin it. Ordinary wagon tools remain; personal cargo and included tools are distinct.

The crossing uses one ordinary Travel Day: **5 lbs food**, no extra generic river strain, no second event. End state: marker 358, food 135, wagon 20, teams 8/8/8, $25, medicine 4, parts 2. June 3 is next morning. Cal's Boundary influenced the decision; if it created a meaningful personal complication, award his available Inspiration award, but he cannot hold more than one. Mae may also qualify through her Promise's cost; no automatic morale penalty for an internal promise's change. If it was a specific NPC promise and that person learns it was broken, apply the core rule then.

Everyone participates in the far-bank conversation. The group can continue or Rest tomorrow; a failure would instead have left them on the near bank with the ferry still available.

## 2. A Rest Day with treatment and preservation

Separate state: July 20, marker 950, hot/dry desert, water 20/60, food 136 cured +40 fresh (caught July 18, expires after July 21 evening), wagon 20/30, teams 8/8/8, medicine 4, parts 2. Cal has HP 4/9, Exhaustion 1, dysentery with 3 marks and damage due today. Others are full HP, no wounds. A sheltered spring is not present; they are resting under canvas with adequate carried water. No random event is due because this is the second day of an already generated delay that permits Rest.

- Thomas uses **Stabilize** on Cal: medicine 4→3; consumes Thomas's Camp Action and daily treatment. Dysentery's 1 HP damage today is prevented. At day's end remove 2 marks, leaving 1; no dysentery recovery roll tonight.
- Mae uses **Jury-rig**: parts 2→1, wagon 20→26. Her free upkeep adds 1, ending 27. No second repair check is allowed today.
- Cal uses **Smoke & Salt**, WIS roll 11: all 40 fresh food becomes cured. Eliza Scouts, WIS total 12, earning the stated next-navigation benefit. Clara uses **Mend & Wash** on Thomas.
- Pay 2.5 lbs food, leaving **173.5 cured, 0 fresh**; hot weather uses 4 gallons, leaving 16. Nobody gains Exhaustion from mere low stores.
- Cal's healing die is 3: heals 4 HP, ending 8/9; fed rest removes Exhaustion 1→0. Other healing clamps at max. No one has a Wound state to check.
- All teams heal 1 to 9; Mae's Ox Whisperer adds 1 to team A, so **10/9/9**.
- Evening: Write or remember grants Inspiration to a character currently lacking it. No Morale increase from that activity, and no extra food cost.

Final changes: food 173.5 cured, water 16, wagon 27, medicine 3, parts 1, teams 10/9/9, Cal 8 HP/Exhaustion 0/dysentery 1 mark. July 21 begins. The stabilization benefit expired; Cal needs the remaining mark cleared and may take disease damage tomorrow unless treated again.

## 3. A three-day leg, without three narrated scenes

Separate healthy state: July 10, marker 910, hot/dry weather newly rolled (three days remaining), two usable teams, food 100 cured, water 60, no wounds or conditions. Standard stance. Event selector 6 gives quiet progress.

Three days: 18 +18 +18 = **54 miles**, food 5 ×3 = **15 lbs**, water 4 ×3 = **12 gallons**. End marker 964, food 85, water 48; next morning July 13, new weather due. No hard-travel strain, no hidden Travel check. A conversation can happen once during the montage; no extra event for talking.

Record that uninterrupted leg in one row, July 10–12, with the totals above. Inventory stays on the wagon sheet. If a prepared obstacle were at 950, day 3 would end there after 14 miles, not 964; remaining miles are not banked. If day 1's event revealed a major choice, stop after resolving day 1 and leave the other two days unspent. A leg does not prepay healing, time, or progress.

## 4. Damage at zero and immediate participation

A traveler with 2 HP takes 4 damage: HP becomes 0 and Healthy→Wounded, no extra step for overflow. Before healing, a separate 1-HP injury advances Wounded→Gravely. Another distinct damage instance while still 0 would be fatal. An able companion instead uses their action to stabilize: HP 1, still Gravely, still unable to act.

The player immediately takes the reserve traveler for the next scene. The patient may be cared for in an allied wagon (agreed, so five people remain in the active wagon) or ride with the group (explicitly count six bodies for food). Only the five active slots provide actions and role benefits. The patient can still have dialogue and a future. No one pays for permission to play.

## 5. Late travel and water warning

One usable team, two travelers at Exhaustion 2, Standard stance, clear weather: 18 ×0.75 ×0.5 =6.75, rounded once → **7 miles/day**. With 40 gallons in heat, water covers 10 days but only 70 miles at this pace. A 200-mile stretch is not safe simply because someone bought two barrels. Show the estimate before departure, then discuss rest, more water, another team, or the guaranteed-source detour.

## 6. A peaceful disagreement

A trader offers 40 lbs cured food for 2 medicine. The party proposes 40 rounds instead of 80. The trader is open to a smaller sale but will not lower the full quantity's price. State terms: 20 lbs for 40 rounds, no roll. If they instead make a plausible new offer the trader is open to, CHA DC 10 can settle that offer. Failure leaves the original terms; the party can decline. No insult roll, standoff, or automatic violence follows.

## 7. Ramp work, a letter, and the departure

Use the river's healthy standalone state, with no treatment, food gifts, or personal-stakes awards. June 2: Eliza and Cal work the ramp; Thomas, Mae, and Clara Rest. Food costs 3.5 lbs, leaving 136.5. Mae's free upkeep restores wagon 22→23; all teams heal 1 and Ox Whisperer adds 1 to A: 10/9/9. Mae sends a $1 letter with Nessa before she leaves this bank that evening, cash 25→24. The resting characters' Camp Actions are unused in this example; choose no reward-bearing evening activity.

June 3: same labor/Rest division. Food 136.5→133, wagon 23→24, teams reach 10/10/10. The ramp agreement is complete; the party has not crossed. Nessa and Mara leave the far bank at dawn June 4. The letter was carried, but no in-person workshop terms were agreed.

June 4: guaranteed crossing, ordinary 5 lbs food, marker 340→358. Food ends **128**, cash **$24**, wagon **24**, teams **10/10/10**, medicine 4, parts 2, ammo 40. No additional event on the three prepared days. Everyone remains full HP and Exhaustion 0. Helping Mercy earns her river intelligence and any eligible personal-stakes award, not an automatic extra Morale reward unless an explicit significant promise was fulfilled. Next morning is June 5 with new weather due. The missed meeting creates future talks, not a destroyed dream.

If only one PC can work and Tomas supplies the other, labor takes three days and crossing is day 4; weather is then newly due in the standalone and can change actual timing. If no PCs can work, use the printed next-ferry aid and immediate substitutions. Do not spend extra days before telling players which option applies.

## 8. A checkpoint inside a leg

Separate state: July 10, marker 910, healthy Standard travel, hot/dry weather with one day left, food 100 cured, water 60, two usable teams. First day's event is quiet. Pay 5 food and 4 water, move 18 miles to 928. Record July 10 or keep its subtotal alongside the leg row: food 95, water 56.

On July 11, roll the scheduled weather: Hail. Players choose sheltered travel at ×0.75. There is no new leg event. July 11–12 each move ceil(18 ×0.75) = 14 miles; water is now the ordinary 2 gallons/day because Hail has no heat modifier. Pay 10 more food and 4 more water. End marker **956**, food **85**, water **52**; July 13 begins with one day of Hail remaining. No wagon damage on the chosen sheltered route. Never apply Hail backward to July 10 or keep charging heat water after the weather changed.

Any ration shortage, expiry, or condition recovery within those dates requires its own daily resolution. One row summarizes the result; it is not permission to omit a check or spend a day after a crisis.

## 9. A new technique changes the route plan

At marker 1,450 the party has two usable teams, clear weather, a usable wagon, one part, and two personal cargo bundles. Mae has Build a Sledge and an able helper. The local path and timber are available. They choose the technique instead of the ordinary one-day separate carry or the risky ledge check: spend the part, keep both bundles, move ceil(18 ×0.5) = **9 miles** to 1,459, and pay 5 lbs ordinary food. No Rest recovery, no ledge damage, no extra clearing day, and no second event. If the site had no timber, that option would be unavailable before the part or chapter use was spent. At a fixed-day finale the technique instead adds its stated labor day and never credits these nine miles.
