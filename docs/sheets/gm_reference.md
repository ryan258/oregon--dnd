# GM reference — v0.10

*Warn them. Offer a choice. Name the price. Then carry it forward.*

**Authority:** [core](../../seed.md). **Start:** [river chapter](../RIVER_CHAPTER.md). **Events:** [chapter d6](../../01_events_and_hazards.md). **Weather:** [seasonal d6](../../06_weather_and_environment.md).

## Table contract

Warn → offer choices → state DC/cost → roll if uncertain → carry the consequence forward. Reliable costly plans work. No roll controls a PC. Every player keeps an active role through incapacity/death. Two consecutive costly random events → next random event is quiet opportunity/message.

## Keep the scene moving

Use the wagon sheet's Next decision panel. Name costs, deadline, person affected, and a player-proposed alternative. Connect one existing scene to a personal opportunity/conflict; record choice → change → return. Rotate first response; players may pass. NPCs follow their announced plans. Give joy and quiet their own space; no obligatory roll or bonus.

## Checks

D20 + modifier vs DC 10 ordinary / 12 difficult / 14 dangerous. Advantage/Disadvantage cancel, never stack; Exhaustion 3+ Disadvantage cannot cancel. Group: more than half full; 1+ but no majority partial; none failure. Incapacitated people excluded. Printed outcomes first; fallback partial completes goal, halves numeric damage/loss upward, full delays. Extended: 3 successes before 2 failures, varied contributors, stated outcomes.

## Time and travel

Keep the [travel procedure card](travel_procedure.md) beside the wagon sheet for checkpoints, daily order, and pause/resume.

Default leg: 3 days, one event on first day. One log row per uninterrupted leg; inventory stays on wagon sheet. Resolve daily effects through the next weather/expiry/health/shortage/scene checkpoint; row splits add no events. Short leg to marker allowed, still one event. Daily weather/health/costs remain. Interrupt before spending unused days. Prepared scene replaces event; fort/delay days no extra events. Daily: reveal → choose → event → action → costs/effects → condition recovery → Rest recovery → watch/camp → date.

Cautious 10 miles (players choose milder of two events); Standard 18; Reckless 26 (GM chooses riskier of two). Two usable teams full; one ×0.75; zero stopped. Two travelers at Exhaustion 2+ multiply ×0.5 once; fewer than two able travelers stops wagon. Apply weather, round once upward. Forced March Cautious/Standard: group CON DC 12, full +50%, partial +25%/failed rollers +1 Exhaustion, failure no bonus/all rollers +1. All attempts hard travel. Hard travel: 1 team loses 1 HP (2 in heatwave).

Event selector d6: 1–3 chapter d6; 4 recurring person; 5 opportunity; 6 quiet. Weather changes every 3 calendar days and chapter entry. Shelter WIS DC 10 / 12 exposed terrain; failed search can become full-day guaranteed shelter-building. Bedding protects travelers from cold, not stock.

## Resources

Food/person: ordinary 1 lb, hard 2, Rest 0.5. Missing full ration +1 Exhaustion; no food at Exhaustion 3+ also 1 HP. Water/party in dry stretches 2 gal or 4 in heat; insufficient day's amount each traveler −1 HP/+1 Exhaustion, one team −2 HP. Fresh cap 100 lbs; expiry acquisition +3 days after evening; cured stores keep.

Hunt full day or half-speed travel: 5 ammo, DEX DC 10/14, yield 60/30 fresh lbs. Take Aim full Hunt Day, 5 ammo, 30 guaranteed with game. Repair STR DC 10: 1d4+2 capped 15, or on success part for 1d6+3 up to 30. Teamster Jury-rig part for 6 guaranteed. One repair/day + Rest upkeep 1. Cargo bundle: 50 food / 1 part / 40 ammo / filled extra barrel / named personal cargo.

## Harm and recovery

At first 0 HP Wounded; separate hit at 0 or return to 0 advances Gravely then Dead. One step/hit. Ordinary stabilization action restores 1 HP. Wounded: physical tasks unavailable at 0, STR/DEX Disadvantage otherwise. Gravely: no actions/checks, can ride. Replacement: immediate, full normal HP, Exhaustion 1, earned techniques, no new supplies or fee. Temporary substitute uses one of five active slots; account for all bodies fed.

Exhaustion 1 −1 checks; 2 physical Disadvantage replaces −1; 3 all Disadvantage uncancelable; 4 max HP half/up; 5 incapacitated; 6 death. **Gain max 1/day, all sources.** Recovery needs full ration. Condition recovery ignores condition/Exhaustion/Morale penalties.

Fed Rest: 1d4+1 HP, −1 Exhaustion, CON DC 10 wound improvement; one Camp Action/able traveler; each living team+1 HP; Teamster upkeep+1. Unfed heal 1 only. One evening activity, see camp module. Ordinary treatment once/day INT DC 10, disease costs 1 medicine, injury free; success halves today's condition damage upward +Advantage on tonight's recovery, expires tonight. Doctor Stabilize on Rest uses action/medicine/treatment, prevents condition damage and removes 2 marks instead of check.

## Meaning and ending

Personal-stakes Inspiration once/character/session, hold 1. Morale 8+ one group Advantage/session; 3–4 disagreement; 1–2 breaking point; 0 safe-camp regroup Rest to 3 or agreed ending. −1 death/known broken promise/first crossing below 50 food or 5 wagon per chapter. +1 chapter endpoint/fulfilled promise/Rest evening. At chapters 2/5 technique. Record favors with names. Finale at 1900 replaces last 100 miles with fixed days. Arrival belongs to players; no random lifespan or involuntary profession.
