# River decision card

## Crossing offers

Show or read the four offers. The standalone begins **June 2 at marker 340** with **$25, 2 spare parts, and 140 lbs of cured food**. Crossing takes one day and moves the wagon to **358**. See the [river chapter](../RIVER_CHAPTER.md) for the full starting state. In the campaign, use the actual dates, supplies, and weather.

| Approach | Earliest crossing | Cost through crossing | Meeting with Mara |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pay for the ferry** | Day 2, June 3 | $10 at boarding and 6 lbs of food if everyone Rests today. Add $10 to pay Mercy's fare. Safe crossing with wagon, teams, and cargo. | Evening of day 2 |
| **Work on the ramp** | Day 3, June 4 | Two days with two able workers each day. 10 lbs of food if everyone else Rests. Earn safe ferry passage for the party and Mercy for $0. | Missed on the posted schedule |
| **Try the upper ford** | Day 1, June 2 | $0 and 4 lbs of food. Group DEX/WIS DC 12. See the outcomes below. | Evening of day 1 if you cross |
| **Prepare a rope line at the ford** | Day 2, June 3 | 1 spare part for anchors and 8 lbs of food. One full party labor day, then a safe crossing. | Evening of day 2 |

Food assumes four travelers, the stated Rest choices, and ordinary rations before Stretch the stores. Mercy's party feeds itself. Waiting, scouting, and labor use days at marker 340.

**Scout the ford first.** Spend a day scouting for Advantage on the group check. The Guide does the scouting. Everyone uses ordinary rations that day and forgoes Rest benefits. Earliest crossing is day 2, with 8 lbs of food through crossing and no cash or parts spent. If you cross, you can meet Mara that evening. Advantage never stacks.

**Ford outcomes.** Each able participant rolls d20 + DEX or WIS modifier based on their contribution. A total at or above the DC succeeds.

- **Majority:** everyone crosses with wagon, teams, and cargo intact.
- **One or more, short of a majority:** cross and lose 2 Wagon HP. Choose one lost cargo bundle or a volunteer taking 1d4 HP damage to protect it.
- **Zero successes:** stay at 340, lose 4 Wagon HP and 2 HP from one team, and pay the day's costs once. Other approaches remain available tomorrow.

A cargo bundle can be 50 lbs of food, 1 part, 40 rounds, a filled extra barrel with its water and capacity, or a named personal bundle.

### What changes the quote

- **Departure.** Nessa and Mara leave the far bank at dawn on day 3, June 4 in the standalone. Mae can discuss workshop terms on either of the first two evenings. Send a $1 letter with Nessa from today's queue to keep a later discussion open. Any partnership still needs agreement.
- **Weather.** Spring showers leave the ferry safe and raise the unprepared ford DC to 13. Thunderstorms hold paid and labor-earned ferries for the weather span. Both ford options stay open in ordinary thunderstorms if the party can move the wagon. A blizzard closes all crossings. Any open crossing is one ordinary Travel Day to marker 358. Waiting can be Rest. Apply the current season's ford DC modifier in campaign play. If weather prevents Nessa's own travel, announce the revised departure before the next commitment.

## Trailmaster reference

### Spend one day at a time

Reveal weather and the deadline. Resolve the action, pay rations and any tracked water, then apply condition damage after declared treatment. Roll each active condition's recovery or apply the Doctor's Stabilize to its target condition. Apply earned Rest recovery, hold camp and watch, and advance the date once. The prepared river scenes take the place of random events.

- **Rest:** stay put. Each resting traveler eats 0.5 lb and gets one Camp Action if able. If fully fed, heal 1d4+1 HP, remove 1 Exhaustion, then roll CON DC 10 to improve one Wound step. If unfed, heal 1 HP only. Each living team heals 1 HP and recovers from lameness. If the Teamster rests, restore 1 Wagon HP alongside the day's one repair action. Observe HP maxima and the 1 Exhaustion gain per traveler per day cap.
- **Ramp labor:** two able PCs work each day and can rotate. Workers eat 1 lb each and forgo Rest. Other travelers can Rest individually. The teams get their normal Rest recovery on labor days. If Mae Rests, add her wagon upkeep and Ox Whisperer bonus to one team. Finish both days to earn the ferry, or stop after the first and pay the normal price on a later day.
- **Ordinary treatment:** one patient and condition daily alongside labor or a Camp Action. The Doctor rolls INT DC 10. Pay 1 medicine for disease before rolling. Injury treatment is free. Success halves that day's damage rounded up and gives Advantage on tonight's recovery. Stabilize uses the same daily treatment.

| Camp Action | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Repair wagon | STR DC 10. Restore 1d4+2 HP up to 15. On success, spend 1 part to restore 1d6+3 instead, up to 30. A Teamster can spend 1 part for a guaranteed 6 HP up to 30 instead of rolling. |
| Preventative maintenance | STR DC 10. Store one nonstacking reduction of 3 HP against the next wagon hit, minimum zero damage. |
| Forage or fish | WIS or DEX DC 10 where available. Each success adds 1d6 lbs fresh food. Up to two travelers try, with others able to help. |
| Intensive care | INT DC 10. Give one patient +2 HP or Advantage on tonight's Wound recovery check. This takes the full action. |
| Stabilize a condition | Doctor spends 1 medicine and their Camp Action. Prevent one patient's condition damage and worsening for today. Remove 2 marks from one condition instead of its recovery check. |
| Stabilize at 0 HP | An able traveler spends their action to restore 1 HP to a patient at zero. The patient's Wound state stays in effect. |
| Scout from camp | WIS DC 10. Learn the next obstacle's warning and gain Advantage on one navigation check there. The full-day ford scouting offer uses its own procedure above. |
| Smoke & Salt | WIS DC 10 with fire and a usable site. Cure up to 100 lbs of chosen fresh food. On failure, cure half the amount chosen, rounded down, and lose the rest of that amount. |
| Mend & Wash | Give one traveler Advantage on their next initial disease resistance check. Store one benefit per traveler. |
| Keep a promise | Do specific work toward the promise. Roll only when the result is uncertain. State any cost up front. |

**Evening.** Invite a short conversation. On a Rest Day choose up to one activity. **Tell the truth:** if at least two characters respond, +1 Morale once per chapter. **Proper meal:** spend one extra Rest ration per traveler for +1 Morale. **Write or remember:** give Inspiration to one character who lacks it. Their personal-stakes award remains available.

**Watch.** Assign three shifts to able travelers. A double shift calls for CON DC 10 the next morning or +1 Exhaustion under the new day's cap. With no watch, skip the early warning of any night threat and present its immediate response choices.

### Weather and the next session

The standalone has Clear and Mild weather on June 2–4. Roll this d6 on June 5 and every three calendar days afterward. Reroll at chapter entry and restart the count. For later months in the campaign, use the [seasonal weather rules](../../06_weather_and_environment.md).

| d6 | April–June weather |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Clear and Mild. No modifier. |
| 4 | Spring Showers. Ford DC 13. |
| 5 | Thunderstorms. Ordinary travel ×0.75, the ferry waits, and exposed camps cannot cure meat. |
| 6 | Late Frost. Each unprotected traveler rolls CON DC 10 nightly or gains 1 Exhaustion. Warm Bedding or substantial shelter protects travelers from the cold. |

**Shelter.** The Guide gets one free search per day at WIS DC 10, or DC 12 in open plains. On failure the party can build shelter with tools and canvas as the day's action, using the day at camp without Rest. For a cold warning first revealed at night, search immediately. On failure emergency cover costs half the next day's mileage.

**Pause at the far bank.** Finish the crossing day's costs, camp, and watch, then advance the date once. On the [trail log](trail_log.md), record the day closed and the next date to play. For example, crossing on June 2 ends at marker 358 with June 3 next and two Clear and Mild days left. If you stop mid-day, mark which steps are already done. Update supplies, health, and agreements on the existing sheets. Earn the first technique at Fort Laramie, marker 500.
