# Travel procedure — v0.10

Keep totals on the wagon sheet and decisions on the trail log. Use the [core rules](../../seed.md) and any printed scene exception.

## Before advancing time

1. **Show the decision.** Name the destination, danger, next safe water, and deadline. Read date, weather and days left, usable teams, able travelers, and anyone at Exhaustion 2+.
2. **Choose Travel, Hunt, Repair, Trade, or Rest.** Choose a stance for Travel. Use a three-day leg unless an obstacle calls for a Focus Day. A leg can end early at a scene or marker.
3. **Mark the earliest checkpoint.** Note the first scene, weather change, food expiry, shortage, health or ability change, or requested decision. Write its date or marker on the wagon sheet.
4. **Attach one event to the first day of the leg, even a short leg.** A prepared scene replaces it. Forts and existing delays add none. Keep a rolled event through a plan change or split log row. Start a new leg after an obstacle ends the current one.

## Resolve through the checkpoint

| Order | Do this | Stop or recalculate when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reveal | Apply weather and disclose danger. Reroll every three calendar days and on chapter entry. Restart the span. | Recalculate water, speed, and exposure when weather changes. |
| Choose / act | Confirm action and stance. Resolve the event, then take the action if compatible. Hunt or forage can feed people today. | If interrupted, finish today. Leave unused leg days unspent. |
| Pay and apply | Pay food and tracked water. Apply strain, weather, and condition effects. Declare treatment before damage. | Resolve shortages, unusable teams, or health changes. Gain at most one Exhaustion per traveler per day. |
| Recover | Roll for each active condition. Doctor Stabilize replaces its target's check. On Rest, apply healing, Exhaustion recovery, and fed wound checks. | Recalculate tomorrow's speed and care after recovery. |
| Close | Hold camp and watch, resolve evening expiry, and advance the date once. Mark weather days used. | Stop at a marker, fort, deadline, or requested decision. Carry no surplus miles past a scene or fort. |

**Batch only identical arithmetic.** With two usable teams, healthy travelers, and unchanged clear weather, three Standard days are 54 miles and 12 lbs food. Roll the event first and track water where required. Resolve changing health daily. Apply new speed only to the days it affects.

## Pause and resume

Tell the table the next checkpoint, expected cost, and what could stop the leg sooner. Ordinary conversation uses the current day and event. Record actual dates, miles, resource changes, and choice → consequence → next appearance.

Write the next playable date and marker, weather days left, unresolved events or delays, and next decision on the [trail log](trail_log.md). Mark completed steps if paused mid-day. Resume with the first unfinished step.
