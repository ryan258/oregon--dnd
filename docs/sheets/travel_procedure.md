# Travel procedure — v0.10

A table card for [core rules](../../seed.md). Keep totals on the wagon sheet and decisions on the trail log. This card summarizes the core; scene-specific exceptions still apply.

## Before advancing time

1. **Show the next decision.** Name the destination/scene marker, visible danger, next safe water source, and any deadline. Read the current date, weather and days remaining, usable teams, able travelers, and travelers at Exhaustion 2+.
2. **Choose the action.** Travel, Hunt, Repair, Trade, or Rest. For Travel choose a stance. Use a three-day leg only while no known obstacle requires a Focus Day. A short leg ending at a scene or marker is allowed.
3. **Mark the earliest checkpoint.** Scene/marker; weather change; fresh-food expiry; shortage; condition damage/recovery; changing ability effect; or a player's requested decision. Write its date or marker in the wagon panel. Never prepay beyond it.
4. **Attach the event.** One event on the first day of a leg, even a short leg. Prepared scene replaces it. Fort and existing-delay days add none. A checkpoint or extra log row does not add an event; an obstacle that ends the leg means a new leg afterward. Keep an already rolled event if the plan changes.

## Resolve through the checkpoint

| Order | Do this | Stop or recalculate when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reveal | Apply today's weather and disclosed danger. Reroll weather every three calendar days and on chapter entry; restart its count. | New weather changes a plan, exposure, water cost, or speed. |
| Choose / event / act | Confirm action and stance; resolve the attached event, then the action if compatible. Hunt/forage yields can feed people today. | An obstacle or event changes the action or causes a crisis. Resolve today, then leave unused leg days unspent. |
| Pay and apply | Pay today's actual food/water. Apply travel strain, weather, and condition effects. Declare treatment before condition damage. | A ration/source is insufficient, a team becomes unusable, or health changes. Apply the printed consequences; gain at most one Exhaustion per traveler/day. |
| Recover | Make each active condition's recovery check. On Rest, then heal HP, remove Exhaustion, and check wound recovery for fed travelers. | Recovery changes tomorrow's speed, care, or ability to act. Doctor Stabilize replaces that condition's check. |
| Close | Resolve expiry after the evening, watch/camp, and advance date once. Mark elapsed weather days. | A marker, deadline, or requested decision has arrived. Carry no surplus miles past a scene or fort. |

**Batch only identical arithmetic.** A healthy Standard day with two usable teams, no modifying weather, and no party Exhaustion penalty is 18 miles and 4 lbs food. Three unchanged days are 54 miles and 12 lbs. Track water only when required. Roll the leg's event before assuming this uninterrupted result; an event can change the forecast.

**Changing health stays daily.** If a traveler has dysentery, apply that day's treatment/damage and recovery before deciding the next day's care and speed. If two travelers began at Exhaustion 2+, a fed Rest Day can change the next day's party speed. Never apply a later recovery backward to earlier miles or charge days the party has not taken.

## Hand the table a decision

Say: “At our current pace, the next checkpoint is ____ on ____. Until then the expected cost is ____. We stop earlier if ____.” This is a forecast, not a guarantee against the disclosed event or changing health.

At a stop, update only changed wagon totals. Log date range, actual miles, resource change, choice → consequence → next appearance. Recalculate the next checkpoint before continuing. Ordinary conversation does not require an extra day or event.

## Pause and resume

Before ending play record: current date/marker; whether today's costs/recovery are complete; weather days remaining; any unresolved event/delay; and the next decision. Resume from that point. Do not roll the same day's event or pay the same day's costs twice.
