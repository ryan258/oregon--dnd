# The journey at a glance

*April in Independence, and two thousand miles of decisions drawn as a single line.*

Version 0.9. **Game progress map, not a geographically accurate route survey.** Distances are intentionally compressed/simplified for the campaign. Each chapter has its own decision rather than a requirement to narrate every day.

```text
APRIL 20
  0  INDEPENDENCE — buy supplies, name dreams
  │  1: THE PLAINS — Mercy's book crate at 120
300  FORT KEARNY — first resupply
  │  2: THE RIVER CROSSINGS — queue and choice at 340
500  FORT LARAMIE — report, bargain, learn technique 1
  │  3: THE ROCKIES APPROACH — Tomas needs a day at 580
700  INDEPENDENCE ROCK — what are you carrying?
  │  4: SOUTH PASS
790  FORK ─── established road ─── FORT BRIDGER 850 ───┐
  │       └── shortcut: 40 actual waterless miles ─────┤
900  DEPARTURE SPRING / REJOIN ◄────────────────────────┘
  │  5: DESERT CROSSING
  │     direct: confirmed-if-scouted spring at 990
  │     detour: +36 actual miles; water after 72 actual miles
1100 FORT HALL — messages, learn technique 2
  │  6: THE SNAKE RIVER — shelter at 1350, ledge at 1450
1600 FORT BOISE — prepare for both finale routes
  │  7: THE LAST MOUNTAINS — cargo choice at 1750
1900 THE DALLES ─── river: 7 days plus preparation/portages
  │             └─ road: 14 days plus chosen delays
2000 ARRIVAL — people, promises, the life you choose
```

The shortcut replaces 110 main-route miles (790–900) with 40 actual miles, saving 70. Chapter 5 detour adds 36 actual miles without route-marker progress for those extra miles. The finale's fixed durations replace 1,900–2,000; no additional mileage rolls there.

## Visible clocks

Nothing on this journey counts down in secret. Four clocks run where the players can read them, and the tension comes from watching them run, not from being surprised by where they got to.

| Clock | What players can see |
| --- | --- |
| Calendar | Current date; weather changes every three calendar days and on chapter entry. July brings heat, September more cold, November severe weather. |
| Next refuge | Marker/distance to next fort or established sheltered site. |
| Water | Gallons ÷ current daily consumption = whole days plus remainder; compare against actual modified travel days and delays. |
| Condition | Recovery marks and tomorrow's damage/effect; no hidden countdown to death. |
| Promise | Person, specific delivery/help, deadline if agreed, known consequence of missing it. |

Mark the party with a coin. Mark graves with a name/date, camps with a dot, and unfinished promises beside their destination. The journey map should gradually become a record of this party rather than a clean diagram everyone shares.
