# 5. Camp Life & Rest Days

The trail is gruelling, but a wagon party cannot survive without stopping. Rest days ("laying over") are essential for recovering Exhaustion, healing the sick, and repairing battered equipment. But rest days are also the heartbeat of the roleplaying experience—this is when characters talk, process grief, and assert their humanity in the face of the wild.

### The Mechanics of Camp

A standard overnight camp happens every evening and allows for limited action (cooking, sleeping, setting watch).

A **Layovers / Rest Day** consumes 24 hours. The party makes zero miles of progress.

- **Consumption:** Food usage is halved (0.5 lbs per person).
- **Recovery:** At the end of a Rest Day, all characters heal 1d4+1 HP, and remove 1 level of Exhaustion. Ox teams recover 1 HP.

### Camp Actions

During a Rest Day, every character can take **one major Camp Action**. You cannot do two things at once.

| Action                  | Relevant Stat      | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :---------------------- | :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Deep Repair**         | STR (DC 13)        | Spend a day under the wagon reinforcing the chassis. Restores 1d6+2 Wagon HP. No parts required.                                                                                      |
| **Preventative Maint.** | STR (DC 12)        | The Teamster greases axles and reinforces yokes. Success means the next "Wagon Breakdown" or rough terrain event is automatically nullified or its damage halved.                     |
| **Foraging/Fishing**    | WIS or DEX (DC 12) | Search the immediate vicinity. Success yields 2d10 lbs of food (berries, roots, fish).                                                                                                |
| **Intensive Care**      | INT (DC 14)        | The Doctor spends all day tending to the sick/wounded. Target character gains Advantage on their next disease saving throw, or recovers an additional 1d6 HP.                         |
| **Brew Tonics**         | INT (DC 13)        | The Doctor gathers herbs to distill preventative tonics. Success yields 1d4 tonics. Anyone drinking one gains Advantage on their next CON save against disease.                       |
| **Scouting Ahead**      | WIS (DC 13)        | Take a horse and ride a few miles forward. Success reveals tomorrow’s terrain or hazards, granting Advantage on the next day's Travel or Navigation check.                            |
| **Mend & Wash**         | DEX (DC 11)        | Fix torn clothes, boil lice, clean weapons. Small comforts matter. Target character gains +1 to their AC (if using combat rules) or automatically succeeds their next minor CON save. |

### The Morale Engine: Evening Activities

Evenings in camp are crucial for party Morale. Once per layover, the party can engage in an **Evening Activity**. The GM should ask the players to briefly roleplay or describe their activity.

- **Campfire Stories / Music:** A character plays a fiddle, jaw harp, or tells a story from back East. **CHA check (DC 12).** _Success:_ The party's Morale increases by 1.
- **Writing in Diaries / Reading Scripture:** Quiet, solitary processing of trauma. **WIS check (DC 11).** _Success:_ The individual character removes 1 condition (fear, despair) or gains Inspiration (Advantage on one roll of their choice the next day).
- **A Proper Hot Meal:** Using 2x the normal food ration and real effort to cook it well. No check required. _Success:_ Everyone removes 1 additional Exhaustion level instantly.

### Watch Duty

During the night, someone must keep the fire lit and watch the stock.

- The night is split into 3 shifts.
- Assigning characters to watch means they still get enough sleep to function.
- **Failing to set a watch:** If an event like _Thief in Camp_, _Wolf Pack_, or _Stolen Stock_ occurs at night, the party is **Surprised** and automatically fails to prevent it.
- **Double Shift:** If a character takes two shifts (because others are sick), they must make a **CON check (DC 13)** in the morning or gain 1 level of Exhaustion.
