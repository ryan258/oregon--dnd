# 2. Backgrounds and learning

*Everyone on this wagon was somebody before Independence. The road does not care, but the party does.*

Your role is what the party needs from you. Your background is the life you had before anybody needed anything from you — the winters already survived, the patients already lost, the wagons already mended. A technique is what the trail itself taught you, and it is worth asking who did the teaching: a person, or a failure.

Version 0.10. Choose one background at creation. Advantages do not stack; a use is expended only when its stated benefit applies. Equipment mentioned here is descriptive unless a benefit explicitly supplies it. A chapter is a numbered campaign chapter, not each play session.

## Guide

*You have been out here before, or you have wanted to be for so long that it amounts to the same thing. The others follow you because someone has to walk in front.*

- **Former Mountain Traveler:** Advantage on checks to find shelter in chapters 3, 4, and 7.
- **Military Scout:** Advantage to spot an approaching physical threat; does not determine another person's intentions or excuse trespass.
- **Driven by a Vision:** Once per chapter, after a Morale loss, explain why you still believe in the journey and restore 1 Morale. Another player may disagree without losing the benefit.
- **Cartographer:** When using Read the ground, also learn one usable sheltered campsite or safe water source on the chosen route. GM must place one before the next known major obstacle; it cannot erase an entire desert.

After chapters 2 and 5, choose one (no repeats):

- **Surveyor:** Read the ground takes half a day rather than one; travel half Standard mileage that day, pay ordinary travel food, and no Rest benefits.
- **Weather Eye:** Once per chapter, before committing to a route, see the next weather roll. Record it and use it at the next scheduled weather change; no reroll.
- **Find a Third Way:** Once per chapter, use Read the ground at a land-route junction (pay its normal time, including Surveyor if learned) to open a viable third land approach: no roll, one additional ordinary Travel Day beyond the shorter offered route, no route-specific damage or required cargo sacrifice. This replaces the junction's immediate terrain obstacle only. Use the known route's sources, not an invented refill; weather, daily supplies, ox/speed modifiers, and existing promises still apply. Does not skip forts, create a route over open water, or replace a fixed-duration finale. GM quotes the actual itinerary and source gaps before the player pays; if the fiction cannot support it, do not spend the use.
- **Bring Them Through:** Once per chapter, on a group navigation check, change one failed participant's result to success after rolls.

## Doctor

*You know the names of things. That is most of what you have, and on a good day it is enough.*

- **Trained in the East:** +1 on ordinary disease treatment checks; not recovery checks.
- **Frontier Herbalist:** Once per chapter, on a Rest Day in suitable vegetation, gain 1 medicine without spending a Camp Action. These abstract supplies are not a claim about real historical cures.
- **Battle Surgeon:** Advantage on ordinary treatment of injuries.
- **Sanitation Keeper:** Once per chapter, give all exposed travelers Advantage on one event's initial disease resistance checks before rolling.

Techniques:

- **Steady Hands:** Ordinary treatment of one injury succeeds without a roll; still uses the once-daily treatment and has its ordinary effects.
- **Bedside Teacher:** On a Rest Day when you use Stabilize, one other traveler can perform ordinary treatment on a different patient, paying normal medicine and making INT DC 10. This grants one extra treatment attempt; it cannot stack on the same day with Care on the Move's extra NPC treatment.
- **Care on the Move:** Once per chapter, ordinary treatment of one patient's broken bone permits that patient to perform physical labor for that day on success; no extra medicine for the injury. This uses the Doctor's normal daily treatment and INT DC 10 (Steady Hands can make it reliable). The patient must be above 0 HP, not Gravely Injured, and below Exhaustion 5. Other penalties still apply, the condition retains its marks, and daily recovery works normally. This can let the patient take part in a rescue, crossing, or packing operation; it grants no extra action. If no such patient is present, the same once-per-chapter use lets the Doctor provide ordinary treatment to one present NPC without consuming the daily treatment reserved for a PC, using the normal cost and check. Do not combine that extra attempt with Bedside Teacher on the same day.
- **Watchful Care:** Once per chapter, reduce a patient's HP damage from one source by 3, minimum zero, after damage is rolled. You must be present and able to help.

## Hunter

*The difference between full rations and short ones is you, walking away from the road alone before dawn.*

- **Sharpshooter:** Ordinary hunting costs 3 ammunition instead of 5; Take Aim still costs 5.
- **Tracker:** Advantage on ordinary hunting checks when recent rain or snow has left readable tracks; the GM discloses availability before commitment.
- **Quiet Step:** Advantage on your checks to approach animals or avoid observation. This does not make everyone else invisible.
- **Trapper:** Once per three-day weather span, spend 1 ammunition and set snares in suitable habitat at camp; gain 5 lbs fresh food next morning without a roll. Mark the use. No yield in terrain without game.

Techniques:

- **Patient Provider:** Take Aim yields 40 rather than 30 lbs.
- **Waste Nothing:** Your Smoke & Salt action cures up to 100 lbs without rolling, when there is a fire and suitable conditions.
- **Provision a Detour:** Once per chapter, choose this instead of Take Aim on a full Hunt Day in established game habitat with fire and conditions suitable for curing. Confirm all three before spending. Spend 5 ammunition and gain 20 lbs cured food, guaranteed; preparing it is included in the day. No Rest benefits, no extra hunt that day; counts as a successful campsite yield and obeys food capacity. This makes a modest ration reserve possible before leaving hunting terrain without another curing day; it creates neither water nor game.
- **Watch the Herd:** Once per chapter, prevent 2 HP damage to one ox team from an animal or environmental threat; cannot prevent hard-travel strain.

## Teamster

*You are the only one who hears the wagon the way it actually sounds. Everyone else finds out when it stops.*

- **Blacksmith's Apprentice:** Advantage on Field Repair checks. Does not add HP to guaranteed Jury-rig.
- **Ox Whisperer:** One living team of your choice restores an extra 1 HP on each Rest Day.
- **Bullwhacker:** Advantage on your checks to control a wagon on a slope or in water.
- **Pack-Master:** Once per chapter, when an event would take a cargo bundle, protect that bundle through careful packing. Cannot cancel a bundle voluntarily offered as payment or traded for a safer route.

Techniques:

- **Lasting Repair:** Jury-rig restores 8 rather than 6 HP.
- **Gentle Driver:** Once per three-day weather span, prevent one day's ordinary 1-HP hard-travel strain. In heat, strain drops from 2 to 1 instead.
- **Build a Sledge:** Once per chapter, at a site with usable timber and ordinary tools, spend 1 part and today's action to build and haul a temporary sledge with one other able traveler. Move up to two cargo bundles past one local land obstacle without rolling or sacrificing them, then the whole party travels half Standard mileage with normal ox/weather/Exhaustion modifiers, rounded once. Ordinary food, no Rest benefits; this replaces that obstacle's bundle-carrying or clearing delay, not both. A usable wagon and at least one usable team are required for the onward travel. Cannot cross open water, bypass a whole finale, prevent unrelated damage, or add permanent wagon capacity. With no timber or viable local path, disclose unavailability before spending. At a fixed-day finale obstacle, the same preparation takes one additional labor day and preserves up to two threatened bundles; no mileage is credited and other finale days still apply.
- **Loadmaster:** When the party must walk, each able traveler can carry 35 rather than 25 lbs of supplies. Personal keepsakes/clothes are still free.

## Advancing between sessions

Nobody on this road levels up. You get better at the thing you already did, usually because it went badly once.

At chapter end, choose whether a personal statement changed. At chapters 2 and 5 also select a technique, copy its exact wording onto the sheet, and describe who taught you or which failure taught you. Never require a particular NPC to survive to unlock advancement. For the standalone river introduction, characters begin with no techniques. They earn their first technique upon reaching Laramie and completing chapter 2. Ending a session or the shorter introductory adventure at the far-bank camp does not grant advancement; resume the remaining road with the same sheets.
