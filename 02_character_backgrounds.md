# 2. Character Backgrounds

The trail is long, and survival requires more than just high stats. Characters have histories, motivations, and specific knacks that give them an edge.

When creating a character, in addition to assigning your Role and rolling your Stats, choose **one** Background Hook associated with your Role. This provides a mechanical benefit or situational advantage.

---

### 1. The Guide

_The compass, the pathfinder, the one who looks at the sky and knows what tomorrow brings._

**Background Hooks (Choose 1):**

- **Former Mountain Man:** You lived in the Rockies for years. You have Advantage on all WIS (Survival) checks made to navigate or find shelter in mountainous terrain (Segments 3, 4, and 7).
- **Military Scout:** You rode with the dragoons out of Fort Leavenworth. You have Advantage on all WIS (Perception) checks to spot ambushes or hostile elements before they strike. Additionally, your scouting experience grants Advantage on Navigation checks made to find safe river crossings (Segments 2 and 6).
- **Driven by a Vision:** You are certain Oregon is promised to you. Once per segment, when party Morale drops mechanically, you can deliver a speech (no check required) to instantly restore 1 point of Morale.
- **Cartographer:** You don't just walk trails, you map them. When faced with a "False Trail" event, you automatically succeed the check to find the correct path.

### 2. The Doctor

_The line between life and a shallow grave. Often formally trained, sometimes just broadly experienced._

**Background Hooks (Choose 1):**

- **Trained in the East:** You attended an actual medical college. When treating Cholera or Typhoid, you add an additional +2 bonus on top of your normal INT modifier.
- **Frontier Herbalist:** You know the green pharmacy. Whenever the party rests in a forested or river-adjacent area, you can automatically gather 1 dose of 'Medicine' for free.
- **Battle Surgeon:** You operated in the Mexican-American War. You have Advantage on checks to treat traumatic injuries (broken bones, gunshot wounds, animal attacks). Once per segment, you may grant Advantage on a single Wound Track recovery check (made at the end of a Rest Day).
- **Quarantine Master:** You are ruthless about sanitation. If disease breaks out in camp, you can mandate preventative measures: everyone else in the party gets Advantage on their initial CON check to resist catching it.

### 3. The Hunter

_The provider. When the flour runs out, the party looks to you._

**Background Hooks (Choose 1):**

- **Sharpshooter:** You don't waste lead. All hunting attempts expend 5 fewer rounds of Ammunition (minimum 1 round). Additionally, you have Advantage on Hunting checks in mountainous or forested terrain (Segments 3, 4, and 7).
- **Tracker:** You can read the dirt like a book. You have Advantage on DEX (Hunting) checks made after a "Rain" or "Snow" event, when tracks are freshest, and you cannot be surprised by large predators.
- **Quiet Step:** You know how to move unseen. If the party needs to sneak past an encounter or steal back taken goods, you can make the stealth roll for the entire group using your modified stat.
- **Trapper:** You set snares overnight. Every morning, roll 1d6. On a 5 or 6, you catch small game worth 15 lbs of food, costing no ammunition.

### 4. The Teamster

_The mechanic, the beast-master, the person who keeps the wheels literally turning._

**Background Hooks (Choose 1):**

- **Blacksmith's Apprentice:** You know iron and wood. You have Advantage on all STR checks made to repair the wagon, and repairs restore +2 extra HP.
- **Ox Whisperer:** You have a bond with the draft animals. Once per segment, when the party engages in **Hard Travel**, the oxen do not take the usual 1 HP strain. Additionally, oxen under your care recover 2 HP per rest day instead of 1.
- **Bullwhacker:** You are fiercely protective of your cargo. You have Advantage on STR checks made to stop the wagon from sliding or overturning on steep grades or river crossings.
- **Pack-Master:** You know how to balance a load perfectly. Your wagon starts the journey with a free $50 worth of hidden, preserved rations (approx. 100 lbs) that doesn't count against your wagon's weight limit or starting budget.
