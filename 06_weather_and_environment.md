# 6. Weather and environment

*You can argue with a ferryman. You can outwork a broken axle. The sky is the one thing at this table that will not be negotiated with.*

Weather is the game's honest antagonist: impersonal, announced in advance, and identical for the prepared and the unprepared except in what it costs them. It turns every three days on the calendar, not because the story wants a blizzard. Read it out loud at dawn, before anyone chooses a stance — the point is never the surprise, it is watching a party decide what a hard sky is worth going out into.

Version 0.10. Roll d6 every three calendar days and on entry to a new chapter (restart the three-day count). Use the current month. Apply the condition on **each** day of its span unless the entry states otherwise. A weather table entry is not an additional random event. Roll recovery/exposure in batches during travel legs, but stop when someone becomes incapacitated or players need a decision.

## Shelter

Canvas keeps rain off supplies. It does not keep November off people. Knowing the difference is most of what survives a cold chapter.

Forts and established substantial shelter protect people, wagon, and teams from weather damage. A normal canvas camp protects belongings from ordinary rain but is not automatically shelter against severe wind/cold. Before hazardous weather, the Guide may seek cover, WIS DC 10 (DC 12 in open desert/plains); one attempt per day, no separate action. Success shelters the whole party and stock for that day/night. Failure reveals the exposure before travel: the party may use its day's action to build secure shelter instead, guaranteed, with zero miles and no Rest benefits that first day. Subsequent days there can be Rest Days. Abandoning that site loses the benefit. If a cold threat is first revealed at night, allow the search immediately. On failure, reveal the exposure and let the party establish emergency cover at the cost of half the following day’s mileage; no Rest benefits for that half day. A threat cannot deny shelter choices merely because its warning arrived late. Tools/canvas are included in the wagon; without them find an established site instead.

**Warm Bedding** protects travelers from cold exposure checks/damage, not animals or wagon. Sheltered people are also protected from cold. A scene may explicitly threaten bedding as a cargo choice; wagon reaching 0 HP does not itself erase the bedding.

## April–June

*Mud, high water, and grass for the teams. The season that lets you believe this will be manageable.*

| d6 | Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Clear and mild | No modifier. |
| 4 | Spring showers | River-check DC +1. No modifier to unrelated travel. |
| 5 | Thunderstorms | Travel ×0.75. An exposed camp cannot cure meat that day. |
| 6 | Late frost | Each unprotected traveler rolls CON DC 10 each night or gains 1 Exhaustion. |

## July–August

*Heat that doubles the water bill. The margin you bought in Independence is spent here, whether or not you notice.*

| d6 | Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Hot and dry | Water use 4 gallons/day where tracked. |
| 3 | Clear with cold nights | Each unprotected traveler rolls CON DC 10 nightly or loses 1 HP. |
| 4 | Dust and wind | Disadvantage on hunting; no morale loss. |
| 5 | Hail | Each day, choose sheltered travel at ×0.75 miles, or normal miles and lose 1 Wagon HP. At camp, seek/build shelter to prevent that day's damage instead. Pay damage at most once per day. |
| 6 | Heatwave | Water use 4 gallons/day. Hard travel costs 2 HP strain to one team instead of 1; each hard-travel participant rolls CON DC 12 or gains 1 Exhaustion. Ordinary travel requires no heat check. |

## September–October

*The nights turn first. Everything after this is a race you did not know you had entered in April.*

| d6 | Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Crisp and clear | No modifier. |
| 3 | Cold rain | Disadvantage on initial disease resistance; treatment/recovery unaffected. |
| 4 | First snow | Travel ×0.75; readable tracks grant Advantage on hunting. |
| 5 | Freezing wind | Each unprotected traveler loses 1 HP nightly. |
| 6 | Snowstorm | Choose wait/rest under shelter, or travel ×0.5 and each unprotected traveler rolls CON DC 12 or loses 1d4 HP. Ox teams do not die automatically. |

## November onward

*If you are reading this table, the calendar has already made a decision about you. Play it straight; it is survivable, and it is not kind.*

Use this table for all later months if the expedition continues; reaching winter is not an undefined state.

| d6 | Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Deep snow | Travel ×0.5, hunting unavailable unless an event establishes game. |
| 3 | Bitter cold | Each unprotected traveler loses 2 HP/day. Each exposed team loses 1 HP/day. |
| 4 | Ice | Travel ×0.5. Reckless travel unavailable; normal travel needs no extra check. |
| 5 | Blizzard | No travel for the three-day span. Seek/build shelter or each exposed traveler and team loses 2 HP/day. Once sheltered, waiting days may be Rest Days. |
| 6 | Brief thaw | Travel ×0.75; river-check DC +2. |

If an event also causes weather damage, show both costs before the choice. No hidden stacking: an event that says it replaces the day's weather replaces it; otherwise both apply. The universal daily Exhaustion cap still holds. A new weather roll can make an old route less attractive; offer reconsideration before charging a new day's costs.
