# 6. Weather & Environment

Weather is the invisible antagonist of the Oregon Trail. A sunny day makes a brutal terrain passable; a sudden freeze makes an easy plateau deadly.

The GM determines the weather based on the current in-game month and the region (Segment) the party is in. Most parties leave Independence in mid-to-late April. A typical journey takes 4 to 6 months.

**If month and segment ever conflict:** Use the table for the **current month**. The segment notes under each table describe where parties *typically* are during that season — they are not hard gates. (Example: If it’s September and the party is still in Segment 3, use the Autumn table anyway.)

### The Weather Roll

Every 3 days in-game, or when transitioning to a new Segment, the GM rolls **1d6** on the relevant Seasonal Weather Table to set the prevailing conditions.

_Note: The Daily Event Tables can override this with sudden, brief storms._

### Shelter (for storms, hail, blizzards)

Some weather results and events reference being **Sheltered**.

- The party is **Sheltered** if they’re at a fort/trading post, in dense trees, in a canyon, under a rock overhang, or any similarly protected spot.
- Otherwise, when a rule says “if not Sheltered” or “without shelter,” the party may try to find and set up cover:
  - **WIS (Survival) check (DC 10)**, usually by the Guide
  - In open terrain where cover is scarce (Plains/Desert), use **DC 12**
  - Success: the party counts as Sheltered for that event/night. Failure: they’re exposed.

**Warm Bedding:** Buffalo robes / heavy blankets. If the party has **Warm Bedding**, characters count as Sheltered for purposes of nighttime cold damage (it does not protect oxen or the wagon). Warm Bedding can be acquired through trading (see the event tables) or purchased at forts. Warm Bedding is lost if the wagon is destroyed, if supplies are dumped overboard (e.g., capsized raft), or if the party is forced to cache supplies and proceed on foot.

### Spring / Early Summer (April – June)

_Segments 1 & 2 (The Plains, River Crossings)_

| d6  | Condition               | Effect                                                                                           |
| :-: | :---------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-3 | **Clear & Mild**        | Baseline conditions. No modifiers. Grass is abundant.                                            |
|  4  | **Spring Showers**      | Steady rain. River crossing DCs increase by +1. Mud makes Travel checks slightly harder (DC +1). |
|  5  | **Heavy Thunderstorms** | Violent storms. If the party travels, multiply miles traveled today by 0.75 (round up). Lightning risks to stock. No campfires tonight. |
|  6  | **Late Frost**          | Surprise temperature drop. CON DC 9 overnight or take 1 level of Exhaustion.                     |

---

### High Summer (July – August)

_Segments 3, 4 & 5 (Rockies, South Pass, Desert)_

| d6  | Condition                 | Effect                                                                                                             |
| :-: | :------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | **Hot & Dry**             | Blistering sun. Water consumption doubles. "Forced March" is impossible without risking heatstroke.                |
|  3  | **Clear / High Altitude** | Cold nights, burning sun. Each night, each character makes a CON check (DC 9). Failure: take 1d4 cold damage. If the party is **Sheltered**, has **Warm Bedding**, or spends +0.5 lbs food per person that night, the check is made with Advantage. |
|  4  | **Dust / High Winds**     | Grit gets in everything. Disadvantage on Perception and Hunting checks. Morale –1 at the end of this 3-day span.   |
|  5  | **Summer Hail**           | Sudden violent ice storms. If not **Sheltered**, the wagon takes 1d4 damage (Wagon HP).                            |
|  6  | **Oppressive Heatwave**   | Temperatures over 100°F (38°C). CON DC 12 daily. Failure: Gain Exhaustion. Oxen take double HP damage from Hard Travel (2 HP instead of 1). |

### Autumn (September – October)

_Segments 6 & 7 (Snake River, Blue Mountains)_
_If the party is still on the trail in late October, panic should set in._

| d6  | Condition          | Effect                                                                                                     |
| :-: | :----------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | **Crisp & Clear**  | Brisk traveling weather. No modifiers, but night fires are mandatory.                                      |
|  3  | **Cold Rains**     | Freezing, soaking rain. Disadvantage on all CON checks against disease. Fires are difficult to keep lit.  |
|  4  | **First Snows**    | Light dusting. Hunting becomes easier (Advantage to track game) but grass for oxen vanishes.               |
|  5  | **Freezing Winds** | Biting chill. Each night, each character takes 1d4 cold damage unless the party is **Sheltered**, has **Warm Bedding**, or spends +0.5 lbs food per person that night. |
|  6  | **Early Blizzard** | Whiteout conditions. Travel is impossible. Stay in camp. If not **Sheltered**, roll 1d20: on 1–3, 1 ox team freezes to death. |

---

### Winter (November – December)

_Segment 7 (Blue Mountains) for slow parties. If your party is still on the trail in November, they are in serious danger._

| d6  | Condition                | Effect                                                                                                     |
| :-: | :----------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | **Heavy Snowfall**       | Deep snow halves travel speed (multiply miles by 0.5, round up). Hunting checks have Disadvantage.          |
|  3  | **Bitter Cold**          | Each character takes **1d6 cold damage** daily unless **Sheltered** with **Warm Bedding**. Shelter alone or Warm Bedding alone reduces damage to 1d4. Oxen lose 1 HP per day. |
|  4  | **Ice Storm**            | Freezing rain coats everything. Travel is impossible for 1 day. The wagon takes 1d4 damage. All DEX checks have Disadvantage for the next 2 days. |
|  5  | **Whiteout Blizzard**    | Travel is impossible. The party must shelter in place for 1d4 days. Each day, characters without shelter make **CON DC 14** or take 1d6 cold damage and gain 1 Exhaustion. |
|  6  | **Dead of Winter**       | Everything compounds. Whiteout conditions (no travel). 1d6 cold damage/day to all unsheltered characters. Oxen lose 2 HP/day. On a d6 roll of 1-2 each day, an ox team freezes to death outright. |
